3. How We Use Your Data

  • Creating and managing your account;
  • Receiving, processing and delivering orders;
  • Communicating with customers (calls, SMS notifications);
  • Improving service quality and fixing bugs;
  • Complying with legal requirements.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • All network traffic is encrypted via HTTPS (TLS).
  • Phone numbers are hashed and stored encrypted.
  • Authentication tokens are stored as hashes.
  • SMS codes are stored encrypted and only temporarily (5 minutes).
  • Data is hosted on servers located in the Republic of Uzbekistan.

6. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for marketing purposes. Exceptions:

  • Sharing necessary order info (name, phone, address) with the courier/service provider;
  • Sharing data with authorities upon a lawful request or court order.

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from such users. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with such data, please contact us — the data will be deleted.
